## Products

The company manufactures 6 main ESP (Electro-Submersible Pump) penetrator systems:

1. GPS-5
   - Sizes: 3/8" or 12mm
   - Working Pressure: 5,000-10,000 PSI
   - Current: 300 AMP
   - Temperature: 350°F
   - Wire: PEEK insulated #4
   - Seal: Metal-to-metal

2. GPS-5-P (Premium Series)
   - Working Pressure: 5,000-10,000 PSI
   - Current: 300 AMP
   - Temperature: 350°F
   - Wire: PEEK insulated #4
   - Seal: Metal-to-metal

3. GPS-5-TC (Tight-Coupled)
   - Working Pressure: 5,000 PSI
   - Current: 300 AMP
   - Temperature: 350°F
   - Seal: Metal-to-metal in bushing assembly

4. GPS-3-LB (Light Barrier)
   - Working Pressure: 3,000 PSI
   - Proof Test: 7,500 PSI
   - Current: 250 AMP
   - Temperature: 350°F

5. GPS-5D (Dual Barrier)
   - Working Pressure: 5,000 PSI
   - Proof Test: 7,500 PSI
   - Current: 250 AMP
   - Special Feature: Dual barrier system

6. GPS-5D1 (Advanced Dual Barrier)
   - Working Pressure: 5,000 PSI
   - Wire: PEEK insulated #4
   - Current: 250 AMP
   - Special Features: No splice below hanger, three-hole 3/8" tube, dual barrier
